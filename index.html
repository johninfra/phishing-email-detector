01 / Message input

Optional identity context

Enter a domain you independently know belongs to the sender. Subdomains are accepted. This is a comparison, not an allowlist.

Imported content is never rendered as an email, executed, or fetched. Nothing is saved automatically.

How to get better evidence

Use your mail client’s “Show original,” “View source,” or “Save as .eml” option. Forwarded or copied messages can lose headers and hidden link destinations.

What this detector can and cannot establish

This is an offline heuristic triage tool. Its 0–100 score measures triggered rules, not a probability. Low scores do not certify safety. It does not check live reputation, follow redirects, query DNS, verify DKIM signatures, scan attachment contents, or inspect images and QR codes.

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC values are unverified claims extracted from headers. Even a reported pass cannot prove the message is trustworthy. Confirm the trusted receiving gateway and original message with your mail administrator.

Supports common multipart MIME, base64 and quoted-printable text. Malformed, encrypted, signed, nested, or unusual messages may be only partially analyzed; inspect coverage notes.
